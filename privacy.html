HypeClip Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

What HypeClip is

HypeClip is a private desktop application for Windows, made and used only by its developer (Dougieee3D) to create highlight videos for his own YouTube channel. It is not sold, published or offered to other users.

HypeClip uses YouTube API Services

HypeClip uses YouTube API Services. By using HypeClip you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

What information HypeClip accesses

When you link your YouTube account (Google sign-in through OAuth 2.0), HypeClip can: upload videos to your own channel, set their thumbnails, post a comment from your channel on your own videos, and read your channel name and the view, like and comment counts of your own videos. It also searches public YouTube videos (titles, tags, publish times and public view counts) to see which titles, tags and posting times are popular.

What HypeClip stores, and where

Everything is stored only on your own computer: the OAuth token (to stay signed in), your channel name and ID, the statistics of your own uploaded videos, and summary counts made from public search results (for example the most common tags and the best posting hours). The summaries are replaced every 12 hours and no YouTube data is kept for more than 30 days, except the statistics of your own videos, which are refreshed regularly. HypeClip has no servers: nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Sharing

HypeClip does not share, sell, rent or disclose any YouTube or Google user data to anyone. It shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking services.

Cookies

HypeClip does not place, access or recognise cookies on your device or browser for tracking. Its own local window uses a single private cookie only to keep other programs on your PC from using it.

Removing access and deleting data

You can disconnect HypeClip at any time in the app (Accounts → Unlink) or at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Unlinking deletes the stored token. Deleting HypeClip's settings folder or uninstalling HypeClip deletes all stored data. You can also ask for help deleting data by emailing douglengyel33@gmail.com; requests are handled within 7 days.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated. Questions: douglengyel33@gmail.com.
