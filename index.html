🎬 HypeClip

A private desktop tool that finds the funniest and most exciting moments in live streams, edits them into highlight clips and helps upload them to the developer's own YouTube channel – always crediting the streamers featured.

Uses YouTube API Services to upload to the developer's own YouTube channel

How it uses YouTube

Signed in with Google (OAuth 2.0), HypeClip uploads finished videos to the channel owner's own channel, sets thumbnails, posts one comment on the owner's new video and reads the statistics of those videos. It shares no data with anyone.

Availability

HypeClip is not distributed – it is used only by its developer.
